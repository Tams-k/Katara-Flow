What the stages mean

Sample entries, shown only to illustrate how the tracker looks
Reference Problem Area Status What it means
KF-0001 Drain blocked by waste Sample area A Reported Received. Waiting for NCWSC to review it.

You see only your own reports. Only NCWSC staff can change a status. You will be notified each time it changes.

At a glance

  • Every report moves through three stages: Reported, In progress and Resolved.
  • Katara Flow covers drainage problems in Nairobi only. It does not cover roads, waste collection or electricity.
  • Built to be light, so it works on a slow connection. Report a drain.

A report that does not disappear

Katara Flow is a web-based system for reporting and tracking drainage problems in Nairobi. A resident reports a blocked or badly kept drain with its location, and the Nairobi City Water and Sewerage Company (NCWSC) takes it through to a result you can see.

Today, residents who report a drainage fault rarely hear back. They cannot tell whether anyone looked at the report, and NCWSC has no structured, location-by-location record of where drains fail again and again. Katara Flow closes that gap with one simple path from report to resolution.

Three stages, always visible

  • 1. Reported

    You describe the problem and tag where it is. Your report is recorded and you can find it again.

  • 2. In progress

    NCWSC has reviewed the report and work on the drain has started.

  • 3. Resolved

    NCWSC confirms in the system that the problem has been dealt with, and you are notified.

Who Katara Flow is for

  • Residents

    Especially people in low-income and flood-prone areas, and the students, traders and commuters who use the same streets. See your own reports and their status.

  • NCWSC

    A structured list of reports by location, so repeat trouble spots show up and maintenance can be planned instead of rushed.

At a glance

  • Every report moves through three stages: Reported, In progress and Resolved.
  • Katara Flow covers drainage problems in Nairobi only. It does not cover roads, waste collection or electricity.
  • Built to be light, so it works on a slow connection. Report a drain.
